Why perform this backup now?

1. Introduction to the Admin Menu: In the top navigation bar, the Admin menu gives you complete control over importing and exporting your data records.

2. Failback Protection: Because this application lives entirely on your personal device, having at least 1 backup point right now ensures you can never lose your foundational answers to Questions 1–5.

What will happen next:

When you click the button below, your browser will download a file named matrix_tracker_backup_YYYY-MM-DD.json.

Notice: Browsers automatically save downloads into your device's Downloads folder. Once downloaded, you may move this file into Google Drive, OneDrive, or any personal folder you are comfortable with.

Why You Should Save This App to Your Device

This is a single-user, local-storage application. It does not send your intimate thoughts to third-party servers. To build habits and record daily periodic assessments seamlessly, you should save this app to your Home Screen or Desktop so it behaves like a native, one-tap application.

Direct Installation Supported!

Your browser can automatically install this app directly to your home screen or desktop.

How to Save (Select your platform):

Apple iOS Safari:

  1. Tap the Share button (box with an upward arrow at the bottom of Safari).
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner. It will now appear on your home screen like any native app.

Android Chrome / Edge / Samsung Internet:

  1. Tap the three dots (⋮) menu in the upper right corner.
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Confirm by tapping "Add" or "Install".

Windows (Chrome or Edge):

  1. In Chrome or Edge, look for the Install icon (desktop screen with arrow) at the right side of the address URL bar.
  2. Alternatively, click the three dots (⋮) > Save and share (or Apps) > Install this site as an app.
  3. This creates a clean desktop icon that launches the tracker in its own window.

Mac (macOS Sonoma Safari or Chrome):

  1. Safari (macOS Sonoma+): Click File in the top menu bar > Add to Dock... > Click Add.
  2. Chrome for Mac: Click the three dots (⋮) > Save and share > Install Page as App.
  3. Launch directly from your Mac Dock or Applications folder anytime.
